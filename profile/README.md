# 🐱 ChitMeo — Smart Accounting & Finance Tools

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **ChitMeo**.

We build simple, reliable, and scalable accounting and financial management tools for individuals, freelancers, and small businesses.

🌐 Website: https://chitmeo.com  
📧 Contact: kyle@chitmeo.com

---

## 🚀 Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify accounting and financial management so that everyone can focus on growing their business instead of dealing with complex paperwork.

We believe that finance tools should be:

- ✅ Easy to use  
- ✅ Transparent  
- ✅ Secure  
- ✅ Affordable

Here are some of our key repositories:

- 📊 **ChitMeo Accounting** — Core accounting system for SMEs
